Who We Are

DermaAI is an AI-driven dermatology platform focused on accurate skin-condition detection and improved patient access to dermatological care. We bring together machine learning, image analysis, and clinical workflows to provide actionable insights and streamline consultations.

Our Mission

To democratize professional skincare guidance by combining AI precision with dermatologist expertise, making personalized care accessible and reliable.

Why Choose Us

  • AI-powered skin image analysis with continuous learning
  • Virtual consultations with licensed dermatologists
  • Personalized routines and product recommendations
  • Lightweight, secure data storage with SQLite

Technology Stack

Backend: Python, Django
Database: SQLite
Frontend: Bootstrap, jQuery, custom templates
AI: Convolutional Neural Networks for image classification

Impact

We aim to reduce time-to-diagnosis, increase reach to underserved areas, and improve patient adherence to treatment plans through intuitive tracking and feedback.
