Feedback Guidelines
Do's
  • Be specific about dates, times, and people
  • Focus on facts and your personal experience
  • Suggest constructive improvements
  • Mention positive experiences too
Don'ts
  • Don't use inappropriate language
  • Avoid personal attacks on staff
  • Don't include sensitive personal information
  • Avoid vague or general comments
